I then defined a final business problem statement"Kerry Enterprise Development Network provides short courses, workshops and one-to-one mentoring for new and growing businesses. Information is fragmented across spreadsheets, forms, email and informal notes, while request states, eligibility, allocation rules, capacity, approvals, responsibilities and outcomes are interpreted inconsistently.
